Vitals & Profile, content-first — the boxes are gone

Both pages rebuilt in the Overview's language: content sits directly on the page, sections part with a hairline rule and whitespace, numbers lead, and the only chrome left is the one gold band and the controls. Same tokens, same type scale, same data as today — nothing new to learn, just less furniture.

Cards → sectionsEvery vital was a bordered box stacked in a column. Now each is a section — eyebrow, its one big figure, the chart, the benchmark — parted by hairlines, exactly like Journal/Holdings on Overview.
Suggestion strips → editorial asidesThe tinted "Worth knowing" strips inside each card become a quiet italic line with a gold lead-in — the same voice as the journal reflections.
The Pulse → a full-bleed bandThe one tinted surface that stays. It runs edge-to-edge (like Diary's "On this day") instead of being a rounded card, and the signal rows below it sit straight on the page.
Perspective card → the page itselfThe gradient box and its inner white panel are gone: the synthesis line, your figure, the distribution curve and the three cohort rows all sit on the paper, hairline-separated.
Settings button → a quiet rowThe big bordered button at the foot of Profile becomes a plain row with a gear and a chevron — the page ends on an open edge.
Unrolled framesEach phone shows the entire page unrolled (as if scrolled end to end), so the full composition can be judged. Light and dark.
