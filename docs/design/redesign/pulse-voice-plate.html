Two voices. Everything spoken — Pulse, insights, questions, verdicts — in Iowan Old Style / Palatino / Georgia italic. Everything measured — figures, labels, vital rows — in the instrument sans with tabular numerals. Figures inside spoken sentences stay sans: €365.000 glints inside the prose.
Why a serif, honestly The product’s moat is stored language — your words, its verdicts, the daily sentence. Giving the spoken layer a real face makes that moat visible: you can tell at a glance whether the app is measuring or speaking. Italic Inter can’t make that distinction; it’s the same material as the buttons. Iowan/Palatino/Georgia ship on iOS — zero font payload, no licensing.
Why the plate Boldness spent in exactly one place. The page stays bone-quiet — hairlines, sans, whitespace — so a single ink object reads as the masthead. Day and night are negatives of each other (ink on bone / leaf on charcoal), which makes the daily sentence feel minted, not tinted. Everything else in the app keeps today’s no-box grammar.
The weather, not a warning When a vital crossed a band overnight, the plate warms to ember, the dateline says what moved, the sentence leads with it, and the moved vital arrives unfolded with its own doorway (“What if you pay down €10.000?”). No red banners, no exclamation — the room just feels different when you walk in. This is the “band-change memory” idea given a body.
One gesture of motion The heartbeat hairline draws itself across the plate once (~1s, play-once, static under reduced-motion) — the app taking the day’s pulse. On an ember day the trace carries two beats. The settle-in stagger from the compact proposal stays; nothing else ever moves.
What survives from the agreed proposal One line per pulse; every line ends in a gold doorway clause; Markets shows the stored euro impact (“≈ +€85”); chips in the what-if hand-off are built from the vitals the Pulse named. Numbers remain deterministic — the redesign changes clothing, never arithmetic.
Cost of adoption Token-level: ~10 CSS variables, one font stack, no new dependencies, no layout rework (the plate reuses the band’s full-bleed slot). The two-voice rule maps 1:1 onto the existing SIGNAL_TEXT_STYLE — one constant changes, every signal row follows. The riskiest piece is taste, not engineering.
